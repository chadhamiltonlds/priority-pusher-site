Quick how-to

Add a task: type it and press return. Start with a number to set its priority: 1 Call mom.

Change a priority: tap the task's number and pick a new one, or press and hold the task and drag it.

Delete: tap the task's text and choose Delete, or press and hold and drag it onto the red bar at the bottom.

Recurring tasks: type it naturally, like every sunday wash sheets, pay rent every 2nd or every 3 days water plants. The Recurring button (top right) shows a calendar of what's coming.

Lock screen widget: press and hold your lock screen → Customize → Lock Screen → add Priority Pusher.

Good to know

Your tasks and notes are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Recurring tasks are added when you open the app on their day. Turn on a reminder in the recurring editor to get a notification on time.
