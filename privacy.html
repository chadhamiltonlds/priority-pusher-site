Privacy Policy

Priority Pusher · Effective October 2026

Priority Pusher does not collect any data.

Your tasks, notes and recurring tasks are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and are shared only with the app's widget on the same device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

If you turn on a reminder for a recurring task, the app asks permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
